# Changelog, Week 46, 2026 (Nov 9 to Nov 15)

---

1. **Fixed: A Region on Aquifers Let In Aquifers From the Next County** (2026-11-10)

    **Component**: `engine/entities/aquifers.ts`, `subdivisionRegionCheck` in `engine/entities/types.ts`

    **Symptom**: Issue #144. "Aquifers in Hancock County" listed 197 aquifers, and 16 of them lie in Washington, Waldo and Penobscot. In Sagadahoc, 15 of its 25 aquifers were from other counties.

    **Root cause**: The region pick checked only the S2 cell. A cell that crosses the county line touches the county, so every aquifer in it passed. Wells got their own check in #96; aquifers had none.

    **Fix**: An aquifer now counts in a region when one of its county subdivisions (towns) rolls up to it. Any overlap counts: an aquifer that crosses the county line belongs to both counties, since its links are within, overlaps and contains alike. Wells and aquifers share the check. Because the check joins an aquifer to each of its towns, an aquifers side with a region is collapsed to one row per aquifer and cell before the join, as a filtered samples side already is (#82).

    **Measured**: on federation, 2026-11-10, all 118 Maine and Illinois counties. The cell check let in 635 of 10,432 county and aquifer pairs (6.1%), in 109 counties; the new check dropped no aquifer the cell check kept. Traces got faster: Hancock "samples within 10 km downstream of aquifers" went from 34s to 6s, Penobscot "aquifers within 10 km downstream of facilities" from 32s to 4s. Report: https://app.superset.sh/page/aquifer-county-filter-6hwnbk

    **Unchanged**: every wells query and every question without an aquifers region (only aquifer shapes moved in the recorded SPARQL), the result cache (no cached question has an aquifers region, so `DATA_VERSION` stays).

    **Files touched**: `engine/entities/aquifers.ts`, `engine/entities/types.ts`, `engine/entities/wells.ts`, `engine/templates/fusedQueries.ts`, `engine/tests/aquiferRegion.test.ts` (new), `engine/tests/__snapshots__/query-shapes.txt`, `docs/DEBUGGING.md`, `GLOSSARY.md`

    **Prevention**: `engine/tests/aquiferRegion.test.ts` checks that a region on aquifers carries the check near and in traces, on either block, and inside the collapsed side; with `LIVE_SPARQL=1` it also checks the Hancock (181) and Sagadahoc (10) counts.
