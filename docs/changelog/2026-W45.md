# Changelog, Week 45, 2026 (Nov 2 to Nov 8)

---

1. **Fixed: Filtered Sample Questions Took Minutes or Failed** (2026-11-04)

    **Component**: `buildFusedWhereBody` and `buildFusedWellQuery` in `engine/templates/fusedQueries.ts`

    **Symptom**: Issue #82. "Samples above 4 ng/L in Penobscot and Knox near (~2 miles) wells in the same counties" failed after 626s: the first step stalled past the 60s request cap and was split by county, and the wells step then ran 533s and gave up. Other questions with a sample filter took 1 to 10 minutes, or came back partial when the 300s step budget ran out.

    **Root cause**: A filtered samples block joined one row per matching measurement, not one per sample point, and the duplicates only collapsed at the final `DISTINCT`. In Kennebec and Androscoggin, 407 sample points entered the 2-hop join as 20,826 rows. The wells step had the same shape twice over: it carried every measurement of every substance on the samples side, and attached the well columns to the whole fan-out of wells and nearby entities.

    **Fix**: A filtered samples side, with its cell, region and any chunk pin, is collapsed to `SELECT DISTINCT ?sp ?cell` before it meets the other side, in near and in both trace orders. The wells step uses the same trimmed samples block as discovery, and collapses its join to one row per well and cell before attaching columns. Unfiltered samples and every other entity type keep their query text. The wells step also stops pretending to split: its query ignores the slice, so each "half" re-sent the identical query until the 300s step budget ran out. A failed wells step now fails on its first refusal instead. Discovery also counts only sample points the map can draw: a point needs a location, and a filtered result must name a chemical. Aggregate rows such as SUM_PFOA_PFOS used to satisfy "above 4 ng/L" while the map, which lists chemicals, never drew those points, so the wells layer showed wells around them (17 such wells on #82, now 0). Measured on the live endpoints, before and after:

    | Question | Before | After |
    |---|---|---|
    | #82, samples above 4 ng/L near wells, Penobscot and Knox | failed, 626s | 23.6s |
    | Facilities within 30 km upstream from samples above 4 ng/L, York | 618s, partial | 10.5s |
    | Samples above 4 ng/L in York, within 30 km downstream of facilities | failed | 31.0s |
    | Facilities upstream from PFOS samples, York | 188.6s | 18.0s |
    | Dashboard: facilities upstream from PFHpA groundwater | 226.8s, partial | 41.5s |
    | Dashboard: samples near landfills and DOD sites, Penobscot | 74.3s | 15.7s |
    | Wells step, water bodies downstream of wells | out of memory | 35.4s |

    Every question that answered both times returned the same rows. The wells layer now loads every well discovery found: 2,622 of 2,622 on #82, where it loaded 2,605 before.

    **Unchanged**: questions with no sample filter and no wells side send the same SPARQL as before (`query-shapes.txt`). Chunking still starts only after a query fails. "Wells upstream from aquifers" still runs out of memory, now asking for 2.7 GB instead of 10.3 GB.

    **Files touched**: `engine/templates/fusedQueries.ts`, `engine/planner.ts`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `scripts/check-query-joins.mts`, `engine/tests/sampleRows.test.ts` (new), `engine/tests/wellRows.test.ts` (new), `engine/tests/questions/critical.ts`, `engine/tests/__snapshots__/query-shapes.txt`, `engine/tests/__snapshots__/question-text.txt`

    **Prevention**: `sampleRows.test.ts` fails if any fused query lets sample measurements reach the spatial join, or if the collapsed side loses its own region or its chunk pin. `wellRows.test.ts` fails if the wells step attaches columns before collapsing, or if any divisible step builds the same query for two slices. Both fail on the code before this change.
