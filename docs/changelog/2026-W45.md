# Changelog, Week 45, 2026 (Nov 2 to Nov 8)

---

1. **Fixed: A Region on Wells Returned Wells Outside It** (2026-11-04)

    **Component**: `featureRegionClause` in `engine/templates/fusedQueries.ts`

    **Symptom**: Reported by David. "Wells near surface water bodies in Hancock County, ME" showed wells across the county line: 35 of 2,264 at 0 cells away, 97 of 5,222 at 1 cell away. Putting the region on block A, block C or both gave the same map at 0 cells away.

    **Root cause**: The region clause only checked that the S2 cell touches the region. A cell on the county line passes, and so does every well in it.

    **Fix**: When a wells block has a region, the well must also link to a county subdivision that rolls up to that region (`?_subdivision kwg-ont:administrativePartOf+ <region> . ?well spatial:connectedTo ?_subdivision`). The cell clause stays. Applied in discovery, well hydration and the chunking probe. Hancock at 0 cells away now returns 2,229 wells, all inside the county; with the region on water bodies only it still returns 2,264, so the two questions now differ. Discovery costs a few seconds more on statewide questions; well hydration runs in the same time, because the check goes after its OPTIONALs (inside the body it took 8s to 23s).

    **Unchanged**: water bodies have no region links in the graph, so a region on them is still cell-based. Sample points and facilities are unchanged for now.

    **Files touched**: `engine/templates/fusedQueries.ts`, `engine/tests/wellRegion.test.ts` (new), `engine/tests/__snapshots__/query-shapes.txt`, `docs/DEBUGGING.md`

    **Prevention**: `engine/tests/wellRegion.test.ts` checks the clause in every wells query with a region and its absence without one. Six of its nine tests fail without the fix.
