# Changelog, Week 47, 2026 (Nov 16 to Nov 22)

---

1. **Fixed: Most Facilities Were Never Shown, and a County Let In Facilities From the Next One** (2026-11-16)

    **Component**: `engine/entities/facilities.ts`, `anyRegionLinkCheck` in `engine/entities/types.ts`, `buildFacilitiesByIri` in `engine/templates/hydrate.ts`, `ui/components/Map/`

    **Symptom**: Issue #145. With no industry filter, "facilities in Cumberland County" found 603 facilities; the graph has 2,203 there. About three of every four facilities have no NAICS industry code and never reached the map. Separately, a county on a facilities side let in facilities from the next county, because only the S2 cell was checked.

    **Root cause**: The facilities query always required a NAICS industry code, filter or not, and so did the details query. The region pick had no check on the facility itself, the leak #96 fixed for wells and #144 for aquifers.

    **Change**: With no industry filter, a facilities side finds every facility with a location, coded or not (Cumberland 2,166, Cook 40,299). A facility with no recognised code shows "Unspecified or Unknown" as its industry in the popup, the hover tooltip and the list of facilities on a shared point. With any industry picked, including Select all, only facilities with a matching code are found, and Select all now reads "facilities with an industry code". A region now also requires the facility's own link to that county or state (Cook 41,150 to 40,299 before the industry change).

    **Unchanged**: questions with an industry filter send the same queries apart from the region check. Marker colours are the same. The Industry dropdown has no "Unspecified or Unknown" option.

    **Cost**: the dashboard questions hold their times. The Cumberland upstream PFHpA question now shows 558 facilities where it showed 140. Illinois facilities near Illinois wells with no industry filter now fails at the wells details step; tracked in #160.

    **Files touched**: `engine/entities/facilities.ts`, `engine/entities/types.ts`, `engine/templates/hydrate.ts`, `engine/cacheKey.ts` (`DATA_VERSION` 2026-11b), `ui/components/Map/facilityIndustry.ts` (new), `ui/components/Map/MapPopup.tsx`, `ui/components/Map/FacilityLayer.tsx`, `ui/components/Map/StackedPopup.tsx`, `docs/DEBUGGING.md`, `docs/QUERY-SWEEP.md` (Part 12), `docs/SCHEMA.md`, `docs/wiki/Dropdowns Industry.md`

    **Prevention**: `engine/tests/facilityRegion.test.ts` checks the region check on every facilities side with a region and none without, the collapsed side, the no-filter bind and the optional industry in details (with `LIVE_SPARQL=1`, the Cumberland and Cook counts); `ui/tests/facilityPopup.test.tsx` and `ui/tests/stackedPopup.test.tsx` check "Unspecified or Unknown"; `engine/tests/questions/critical.ts` adds facilities in Cook near Cook wells with no filter.
