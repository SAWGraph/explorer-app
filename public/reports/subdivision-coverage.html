Subdivision link coverage

Which entity types are tied to the county subdivision (town, township or census division) they sit in. The region filter can only be exact for those types. Measured live on federation, 7 Nov 2026, issue #123.

Share linked, whole graph

Aquifers and wells are linked; water bodies and stream flowlines have no links at all.

How much of each state is linked

Of the entities placed in a state, the share tied to a town. Facilities are near 100% in every state but Alabama, Kansas and DC; samples run from 99.9% (Maine) to 0% (Ohio).

Share = linked to a subdivision ÷ (linked to a subdivision + located only by S2 cell)
Entities with neither are in no state and are left out. An entity located only by S2 cell on a state line counts in both states.

0% 100%  ·  hatched: no data in that state

Why the link matters (schematic)

A cell on the county line touches both counties, so the cell check lets in the well next door; the subdivision link does not.
